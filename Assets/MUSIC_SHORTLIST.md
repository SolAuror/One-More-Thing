## Suggested Unity prototype

Audition the tracks against movement, message notifications and the house's task sounds before choosing one. For a first dynamic-music pass, try the **Melodic EDM Loops** variants at a low level: base loop while more than two minutes remain, loopy variant below two minutes, and skippy or bright variant below one minute. Change at a loop boundary or use a short crossfade, and leave the last few seconds clear enough for the clock and interactions. The tracks' descriptions support this proposal; their fit still needs listening and playtesting.

Keep the alarm and message sounds distinct from the music. Avoid using the music to imply that a player with ADHD must always feel frantic; the pressure is a progression in this particular morning.
